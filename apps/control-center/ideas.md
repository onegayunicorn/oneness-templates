# ONENESS Control Center Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Observatory Console
Very dark operational interface with luminous signal accents, telemetry-inspired charts, and a control-room atmosphere.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Paper Atlas
Light editorial control center with warm paper tones, ink-like typography, and modular cards inspired by a field notebook.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Mineral Signal
A high-contrast graphite and mineral-green operations console that treats capability composition as a navigable systems map rather than a collection of forms.
Probability: 0.03

## Selected approach: Mineral Signal

### Design Movement
Contemporary Swiss information design blended with industrial wayfinding and calm enterprise software ergonomics.

### Core Principles
1. **Operational clarity:** every screen foregrounds status, provenance, and next action.
2. **Structured asymmetry:** a persistent rail anchors the interface while content flows in offset editorial bands rather than a centered dashboard grid.
3. **Material depth:** graphite surfaces, mineral highlights, fine hairlines, and restrained shadows create a tactile console without neon excess.
4. **Visible boundaries:** generated, validated, preview, approved, and production states remain visually distinct.

### Color Philosophy
Graphite is the quiet working field. Mineral green is the ownable signal of healthy composition and verified provenance. Warm parchment is reserved for high-value reading surfaces and documentation-like details. Rust is used sparingly for unresolved dependencies and security attention, never as decoration.

### Layout Paradigm
A fixed, narrow left rail establishes the control plane. The main workspace uses a wide asymmetric content column with a right-side context strip for live status, provenance, and next actions. On mobile, the rail becomes a bottom command dock and the context strip collapses into an inline status band.

### Signature Elements
1. A vertical mineral-green signal rule beside page titles and active navigation.
2. Capability dependency paths drawn as thin stepped connectors with circular nodes.
3. Small uppercase status labels paired with compact monospace provenance strings.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every click should answer one of three questions: what changed, what is blocked, or what can I do next? Primary actions are explicit and anchored near the relevant plan or artifact. Hover states reveal context, while destructive or future-only actions remain clearly gated.

### Animation
Use 160–220ms ease-out transitions for navigation, cards, and state changes. Status nodes may softly pulse only when active. Dependency paths reveal left-to-right in short staggered steps. Respect reduced motion and never animate layout dimensions.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for headings and navigation, **IBM Plex Sans** for body copy, and **IBM Plex Mono** for identifiers, statuses, and provenance. Display headings use tight tracking and a restrained italic accent only for section eyebrows.

### Brand Essence
ONENESS Control Center is the operational workspace for composing, generating, and governing capability-driven applications; it is different because it makes platform boundaries legible.
Personality: precise, grounded, quietly ambitious.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and system-aware. CTAs name the actual operation, never generic filler. Microcopy distinguishes current capability from future intent.

Example lines:
- “Compose the next verified system.”
- “This module is visible in the roadmap, not available in the runtime.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact wordmark paired with a custom six-node “O” symbol: two offset rings joined by a single vertical signal line, expressing pathways converging into one platform.

### Signature Brand Color
Mineral Green — `#7CFFB2`, used as a precise signal color against graphite rather than as a broad wash.
